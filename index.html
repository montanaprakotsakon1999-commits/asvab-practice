How the AFQT is built

Word Knowledge and Paragraph Comprehension are added together and converted to a single Verbal Expression (VE) score. Then: raw AFQT = 2 × VE + Arithmetic Reasoning + Mathematics Knowledge. That raw number is converted to a percentile from 1 to 99 — a 50 means you did as well as or better than half of the reference group. Categories run from I (93–99) down to V (1–9); most services want Category IIIA or higher, and applicants without a high school diploma need at least 31.
